# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in heyListen!

**Pull requests aren't accepted.** heyListen is a personal project with a deliberately small scope, and I want to keep it that way.

**Bug reports and ideas are very welcome.** [Open an issue](https://github.com/nikolaia/heylisten/issues) with:
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- your macOS version, Mac model, and which meeting app you used
- for recording problems, the meeting's `recorder.log` (see *Debugging* in the README). Leave out anything from your meetings you don't want public; transcripts and audio are never needed.

**Want something custom?** Fork the repo and make it yours. It's [MIT-licensed](LICENSE).
